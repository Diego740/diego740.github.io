1. Controller

The MyBudget application (the "App") is provided by Diego Aranda Gómez (the "Controller"), who can be contacted at diegoaranda.dev@gmail.com.

2. Applicable law

This policy is governed by Regulation (EU) 2016/679, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and by Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 on Personal Data Protection and the Guarantee of Digital Rights (LOPDGDD).

3. General principle: the App collects no data

The App does not collect any personal data, does not transmit it to the Controller and does not store it on the Controller's servers. The Controller has no servers and no access to the information you enter in the App.

The financial information you record is stored exclusively on your device and, if you so choose, in your personal iCloud account. You carry out that processing in the course of a purely personal or household activity, so the Controller does not act as controller of that data.

No registration or account is required to use the App.

4. Information stored on your device

The App stores on your device the information you enter: accounts, transactions, categories, budgets, recurring transactions, savings goals and preferences. This data is protected by iOS encryption and cannot be accessed while the device is locked.

5. iCloud sync

If iCloud is enabled, the App syncs your information across your devices through the iCloud (CloudKit) service provided by Apple Inc. The data is stored in the private database of your iCloud account, to which the Controller has no access.

Fields with personal content (names, amounts, notes, dates, currencies, exchange rates, IBAN, bank and payment method) are end-to-end encrypted on your device before they are sent, so that not even Apple can read them. Certain technical data is not encrypted and is visible to Apple: the number of records and their creation and modification dates, the relationships between records, and values with no personal content, such as the transaction type (expense, income or transfer), the frequency of a recurring transaction, or a category's icon and color.

If you lose access to your iCloud Keychain, the encrypted fields cannot be recovered from iCloud; the copy on your device is not affected. Your use of iCloud is governed by Apple's terms and privacy policy. If you do not use iCloud, the App works normally and your data is stored only on your device.

6. Face ID, Touch ID and device passcode

The App may request device authentication when it opens. The verification is performed by iOS: the App only receives the result (success or failure) and never accesses your biometric data.

7. Widgets, Siri, Shortcuts and Control Center

Widgets display summary figures on the Home Screen or Lock Screen. To do so, the App keeps a copy of those figures on your device. Anyone who can see your screen can see them, unless you enable the widget's privacy mode, which hides amounts.

The quick expense action available in Siri, Shortcuts and Control Center only opens the App. If you use Siri, Apple processes your voice under its own privacy policy.

8. Data export

The App lets you export your transactions to a CSV file. The export only takes place at your request, and you decide where to save the file or with whom to share it.

9. What the App does not do

  • It does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • It does not include third-party libraries or services.
  • It does not connect to servers of the Controller or of third parties, except Apple's iCloud when you have it enabled.
  • It does not profile you or make automated decisions about you.
  • It does not sell, transfer or share data.

10. Communications with support

This is the only case in which the Controller processes your personal data. If you write to diegoaranda.dev@gmail.com:

  • Data processed: your email address, your name if you provide it, and the content of your message.
  • Purpose: to handle your query, issue or suggestion.
  • Legal basis: your consent, given by sending the message (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).
  • Retention: for as long as necessary to handle your query and, at most, twelve months from the last communication, unless a legal obligation requires longer retention.
  • Recipients: your data is not disclosed to third parties. Email is managed through Google's Gmail service, which may store data outside the European Economic Area. Google LLC participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, recognized by the European Commission as an adequate safeguard.

Please do not include financial data in your messages.

11. Your rights

Regarding the data processed under section 10, you may at any time exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and data portability, and withdraw your consent, by writing to diegoaranda.dev@gmail.com. You will receive a reply within one month at the latest.

If you believe your rights have not been respected, you may lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) or with the supervisory authority of your country of residence.

The data you store in the App remains under your exclusive control: you can view, modify or delete it at any time from the App itself and from your iCloud account.

12. How to delete your data

  • On your device: deleting the App deletes the data stored on it.
  • In iCloud: in iOS Settings, tap your name, then iCloud and storage management, select MyBudget and delete its data. This action cannot be undone.

13. Minors

The App does not collect data from any user, including minors. If you are under 14, do not send messages to support without the consent of your parents or legal guardians.

14. Changes

The Controller may update this policy. Any change affecting data processing will be published on this page, with its update date, before it takes effect.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does my data sync across my devices?

Yes, provided iCloud is enabled and you are signed in with the same account on all of them. If the App's Settings shows an iCloud notice, your data is only being stored on that device.

Can the developer see my data?

No. The App has no servers: your data is stored on your device and in your private iCloud account, and personal information is end-to-end encrypted.

I have a new iPhone. How do I get my data back?

If you used iCloud, sign in with the same account, install the App and wait for sync to complete. If you did not use iCloud, your data was only stored on the previous device.

How do I back up my data?

You can export your transactions to a CSV file from the App. Note that the CSV only includes transactions; it does not include accounts, budgets, recurring transactions or preferences.

How do I hide amounts in the widgets?

Enable privacy mode in the widget's settings.

How do I delete all my data?

Delete the App from your device and, if you used iCloud, also delete its data in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → storage management → MyBudget. This action cannot be undone.

The App does not recognize my Face ID.

You can use your device passcode. The verification is performed by iOS; the App never accesses your biometric data.
