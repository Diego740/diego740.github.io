Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Location Access

GeoAlarm requests access to your precise location to monitor the distance to your destination and trigger the alarm upon arrival. This data is processed solely on your device and is never transmitted to any external server.

Trip History

Your trip history is stored locally on your device using SwiftData. This data is not shared with third parties, is not uploaded to the cloud, and is not accessible to anyone else.

No Third Parties

GeoAlarm does not integrate analytics services, ad networks, crash reporters, or any third-party SDKs that collect personal data. What happens on your device stays on your device.

Your Rights

You can delete your trip history at any time from within the app. Since no data is stored on remote servers, there is nothing to request externally. For any questions about your rights under GDPR, contact us at the address below.
